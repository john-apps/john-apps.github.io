Introduction

Resettr ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using Resettr, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Profile information such as your goals and lifestyle preferences (collected during onboarding)

Providing this information is optional in some cases, but certain features may require it.

Habit and Progress Data

As part of the core functionality of Resettr, we may collect:

  • Daily task completions and skips
  • Habit tracking and streak data
  • Program progress history

This data is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide tracking, insights, and personalized features within the app.

Usage and Device Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the app, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

This data helps us improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS, Resettr uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be prompted with a system dialog asking for your permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

If you grant permission, we may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to:

  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Attribute app installs to specific marketing channels
  • Personalize your experience and improve our services

If you deny permission, no cross-app tracking will occur and your IDFA will not be collected. You can change this setting at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Generate and deliver your personalized habit-building program
  • Improve app features, accuracy, and usability
  • Measure advertising and marketing effectiveness (with your consent)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information only in the following situations:

  • With your consent — when you explicitly allow it (e.g., granting ATT permission)

Data Storage

Resettr is designed as an offline-first application. Your habit data, task completions, and program progress are stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

Certain data — such as subscription status and attribution data — may be processed by third-party services (Superwall and AppsFlyer) and stored on their servers.

Data Security

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
